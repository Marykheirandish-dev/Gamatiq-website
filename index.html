The problem

Two decisions can carry the same score and completely different risk.

A model gives an applicant an 86% approval score. But is that 86% built on solid, verified data — or on a self-reported income field, a flagged document, or a test with a known error rate? Today, both look identical on the screen. Your team can't tell which decisions deserve a second look, so they either review everything or trust everything. Both are expensive.

Wasted review capacity

You review the wrong cases

Fraud and underwriting queues are full of clean cases that never needed a human, while the genuinely fragile decisions slip through unexamined.

Silent losses

Bad inputs become bad prices

Decisions built on unreliable inputs turn into mispriced policies, wrong credit lines, and shipped defects, with losses that only surface months later.

Regulatory exposure

"How confident was the model?"

Auditors and regulators increasingly want a per-decision answer to why a model was trusted. Aggregate accuracy no longer satisfies the question.

How it works

A trustworthiness score for every decision your model makes.

GAMATIQ sits between your model and your decision. It measures how much of each prediction's uncertainty comes from errors in the input data — versus the model itself — and routes accordingly. Nothing about your model changes.

  • 1

    Send us the decision

    Pass your model's prediction, the inputs behind it, and the known error rates of those inputs — through a single API call.

  • 2

    We separate the uncertainty

    GAMATIQ isolates the uncertainty caused by unreliable inputs from the model's own uncertainty — and pinpoints which field is responsible.

  • 3

    You act with confidence

    Reliable decisions flow through automatically. Fragile ones are flagged for review, each with a plain reason your team and auditors can act on.

Why you can trust it

GAMATIQ is powered by statistically rigorous probabilistic models that quantify decision uncertainty.

GAMATIQ is built on a published mathematical framework and is backed by mathematical proof. It is tested on real and simulated data.

Mathematical foundation

Proven, not tuned

The method rests on formal mathematical results: a variation-ratio measure of prediction uncertainty, derived for binary classifiers with two distinct sources of uncertainty, the model itself and errors in its inputs. Every reliability score traces back to a proof.

Derivations you can verify, not a black box
Tested on real data

Validated where stakes are highest

Applied to real patient data in a clinical outcome-prediction study, where the error rate of every input is known. The framework pinpointed the exact cases where an unreliable input could change the decision.
